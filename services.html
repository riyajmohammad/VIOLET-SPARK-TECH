Services

Services & Annual STEM Experience

Violet Spark Technologies offers the most engaging STEM service portfolio in Lucknow: academic classes, workshops, winter and summer camps, and the flagship Robo Hackathon. Our programs combine practical engineering, mentor-led projects and certificate-backed achievement.

Academic STEM Classes

Structured academic programs across Robotics, AI, IoT, Space Technology, Chemical Engineering, Civil Engineering and Block Coding, designed for learners from KG to B.Tech.

  • • Project-based learning with real engineering outcomes
  • • Curriculum-aligned sessions for schools and individual learners
  • • Live guidance from experienced engineers and mentors

Workshops

High-impact workshops for schools, institutions and young innovators. Each workshop delivers hands-on STEM learning and practical technology demonstrations.

  • • Robotics, AI, IoT and engineering project modules
  • • Short-format, engaging sessions for student groups
  • • Teacher training and school-ready delivery options

Winter Camp & Summer Camp

Seasonal STEM camps that immerse learners in collaborative projects, innovation challenges and practical experiments during winter and summer breaks.

  • • Teamwork and project showcase experiences
  • • Camp-based learning in robotics, coding and science
  • • Certificate of participation for all students

Annual Robo Hackathon

Our flagship event invites students to conceive, prototype and present technology-led solutions. It is a platform for innovation, teamwork and recognition.

  • • Every participant receives a certificate
  • • Top 3 teams win cash prizes, mementos and winner certificates
  • • Recognized as a leading STEM hackathon in Uttar Pradesh

Why Choose Violet Spark Services?
