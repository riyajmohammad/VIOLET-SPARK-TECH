About Us

Igniting curiosity, building future-ready innovators.

At Violet Spark Technologies Pvt. Ltd., we believe in igniting curiosity and shaping the innovators of tomorrow. As a STEM-based education provider, we bring together real engineers, real-world projects, and hands-on learning experiences to empower students with future-ready skills.

Our mission is simple yet powerful: to make STEM education accessible, practical, and inspiring for learners everywhere. By blending theory with hands-on projects, we prepare students not just to learn, but to lead in the rapidly evolving world of technology.

Our commitment

We deliver expert-led STEM programs, camps and competitions that engage every student, drive innovation and build confidence through achievement.

  • • Academic STEM classes from KG to B.Tech
  • • Workshops, winter camps and summer camps
  • • Flagship annual STEM Robo Hackathon
  • • Certificates for every participant and top prizes for winners

Annual Robo Hackathon

Every year, we proudly organize the STEM Robo Hackathon, a flagship event where creativity meets technology and young minds design real solutions for practical challenges.
